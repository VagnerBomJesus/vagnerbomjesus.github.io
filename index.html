About me

My name is Vagner Bom Jesus. I am a Software Engineer from Portugal, graduate of the Instituto Politécnico da Guarda, working mainly with Flutter and Dart to build cross-platform mobile and web applications.

I created The Biomimicry Database (TBDB) and I publish academic and technical writing on Medium and Google Scholar. I am passionate about information security, clean architecture and turning research into products people actually use.

How I work

Every project follows the same three steps: from the first sketch to a product that stays secure and up to date.

  1. 01

    Design

    I analyse the requirements, model the data and design the interface based on your needs and suggestions. I can also start from an existing design by consulting with you.

  2. 02

    Development

    Based on the approved design, I build the application with Flutter, Dart and modern web technologies, fully functional, responsive and version-controlled with Git.

  3. 03

    Security & Maintenance

    In case of problems or the need for changes, I introduce new functionalities, review the code for security issues (OWASP) and keep dependencies up to date.
